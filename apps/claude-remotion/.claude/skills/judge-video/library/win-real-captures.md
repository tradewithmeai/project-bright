# WIN · real-captures (capture-first product footage)

- **verdict:** WIN (the turnaround from `fail-recreated-not-real`)
- **surface:** the demo spine (RapidFlow) and product beats
- **human's words:** "that is a pass… a little blury but that could have been capture" · "is it all programable?"
- **date / version:** the rebuild after the first cut was rejected

## What worked
Real Playwright captures of the live product (`mygov-hackathon.vercel.app/source-lens`, voting, gender, PublicWhip, WriteToThem) composited **under a virtual camera** (`GuidedZoom`) with frame-based overlays + captions. The product on screen is the *actual* product, so it reads as real, not a mockup — directly curing the rejected first cut. Slight capture blur was acceptable *because* it was clearly real footage.

## Why it's context, not a target
This records the path that works for *product screens* — it is the positive counterpart of `fail-recreated-not-real`, and it's why the live `repo-ui-to-motion` skill is capture-first. It is NOT a mandate to capture everything: terminals and non-capturable dev tools are still legitimately recreated (see `terminal-recreation`). The judge should not penalise a *novel* good cut for not resembling this — wins are floors of "what's known to work," not ceilings.
