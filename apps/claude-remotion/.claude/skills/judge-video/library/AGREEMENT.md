# Agreement Ledger — predicted vs actual human verdict

The judge's regression test (Lock 3). Each judged cut logs a row with the **predicted** verdict; when the human records their **actual** verdict, the row is completed and the agreement rate recomputed. The rate is only meaningful on **held-out** verdicts the judge had not seen — early rows are calibration, not proof.

**Trust gate:** until the agreement rate is high over enough held-out rows, the human reviews every cut (the judge only pre-filters / pre-warns). As the rate climbs, the human moves to spot-check, then exception-only. The judge never auto-approves at any stage.

**Verdict authority (binding — see [../VERDICT_AUTHORITY.md](../VERDICT_AUTHORITY.md)).** When `actual` (human) contradicts `predicted` (judge), `agree?` = no and the **human verdict is the truth** — the row is the judge's MISS, logged and learned from, never treated as a wrong human. A verdict is filtered out only by a separate, **disagreement-blind** provenance gate (not-engaged / empty / stale / self-contradictory), and even then it is **quarantined for human re-confirmation**, never silently dropped. Disagreements are the prize signal — a high agree-rate with zero misses is a red flag the filter may be eating dissent, not proof the judge is good.
